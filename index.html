Understanding Your Elemental Landscape

Your elemental nature isn't meant to be divided into equal parts. Each of us carries a unique relationship with Fire, Earth, Air, and Water.

These results offer a snapshot of how those energies may be expressing themselves in your life right now. A stronger presence doesn't necessarily mean an element is in harmony, just as a quieter presence doesn't mean something is lacking.

The invitation is to become curious about your own elemental landscape — not to correct it, but to understand it more deeply.

The percentages show each element's share of your total answers to this quiz. They aren't percentages of your personality or birth-chart constitution, and 25% each is not a target.
